The cellar

eighty labels, nearly all from Lazio

A dream of Rome in your glass

Wine at Alfredo's

first first

For sixty years the wine here was loose from the Castelli. Then Lazio began making serious wine, and the cellar changed: Cesanese from Piglio and Olevano, Bellone from the Anzio coast, Malvasia puntinata from Cori, Grechetto from the upper Viterbo hills.

We keep few bottles per label and buy straight from the growers, nearly all less than an hour from here. From outside the region only what we really need: a mountain red or two, a Verdicchio, the sparkling wines.

Eight wines by the glass change every week: they are written on the blackboard in the room. If you prefer, our Marco brings you two tastes and you choose.
