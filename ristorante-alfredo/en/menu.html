The menu

seasonal, handwritten every morning

Roman cooking

Few things, done well

The menu changes with the market: what you find here is what we usually serve, but every day the blackboard in the room adds two or three dishes born from the morning's shopping.

Starters to begin

Crostini con mortadella e stracciatella 12 € Toasted Lariano bread, Apulian stracciatella, mortadella from Bologna, chopped Bronte pistachios.
Bruschette con pomodoro e burrata 10 € Four pieces: hand-cut tomatoes, burrata, basil pesto, new oil.
Mozzarella di bufala, pomodori e basilico vegetarian 11 € Campanian buffalo mozzarella arrived this morning, piennolo tomatoes, basil and Canino oil.
Carciofi alla giudia in season 9 € Roman cimaroli, double fried, Cervia salt. November to April.
Supplì al telefono 8 € Three pieces, rice in beef ragù, a heart of Amaseno mozzarella.
Puntarelle alla romana vegan 8 € Cantabrian anchovy sauce, garlic, white wine vinegar.
Fiori di zucca fritti 9 € Craft beer batter, anchovy, mozzarella. Four pieces.
Tagliere del Lazio 16 € Guanciale from Amatrice, coppiette, pecorino from Picinisco, Cesanese grape jam.

Pasta made in the house

Spaghetti allo scoglio 19 € Mussels, clams and Tyrrhenian prawns, cherry tomatoes, garlic and parsley. Fridays and Saturdays.
Le fettuccine di Alfredo our signature 16 € Only cream butter and thirty-month parmesan, stirred at your table in front of you. The dish it all started from.
Risotto allo zafferano 15 € Carnaroli rice, saffron threads, finished with parmesan. Minimum two people, twenty minutes' wait.
Penne al pomodoro e basilico vegetarian 12 € Sermoneta tomatoes, basil, a thread of raw oil. The children's dish, and the one for people in a hurry.
Fettuccine al ragù 14 € Egg pasta rolled in the morning, beef and pork ragù cooked three hours.
Rigatoni alla gricia 14 € The fourth Roman pasta, no egg and no tomato: guanciale, pecorino, pepper.
Ravioli di ricotta e spinaci, burro e salvia vegetarian 14 € Roman sheep's ricotta, brown butter, sage, thirty-month parmesan.
Gnocchi di patate al pomodoro e basilico Thursday 13 € Thursday is gnocchi day, as Rome demands: Avezzano potatoes, tomato sauce cooked two hours, pecorino on the side.
Tortellini in brodo di cappone winter 16 € The Sunday dish from November to February: hand-made tortellini, capon broth cooked twice.
Tonnarelli cacio e pepe 14 € Pecorino romano aged eight months, Sarawak pepper toasted to order.
Rigatoni alla carbonara 15 € Crisp guanciale, egg yolks, pecorino. No cream, ever.
Bucatini all'amatriciana 14 € Sermoneta tomatoes, guanciale, pecorino, a touch of chilli.
Fettuccine al ragù bianco d'agnello 16 € Lamb from the Simbruini mountains cooked four hours, thyme, lemon zest.
Minestra di broccoli e arzilla 15 € The Roman Friday soup, with skate broth and Roman broccoli.

Main courses from the fire

Grigliata di calamari e gamberoni 24 € On the griddle, parsley and lemon, with fried potatoes and a small salad.
Fritto di calamari e gamberi 19 € Tyrrhenian fish, semolina flour, lemon. Crisp, never greasy.
Coda alla vaccinara 22 € Six hours of cooking, celery, bitter cocoa and pine nuts. Alfredo's own dish.
Saltimbocca alla romana 21 € Veal, prosciutto from Bassiano, sage, white wine from the Castelli.
Abbacchio a scottadito 24 € Grilled chops, rosemary potatoes. For two, if you like.
Trippa alla romana 18 € Tomato sauce, Roman mint, pecorino. On Saturdays, as tradition has it.
Baccalà in guazzetto Friday 20 € Pine nuts, raisins, piennolo tomatoes, toasted bread.
