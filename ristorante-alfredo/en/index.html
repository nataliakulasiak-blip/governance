Our story

A trattoria that became home

In 1962 Alfredo Sabatini opened seven tables on Via Principe Amedeo, a few steps from Termini station and the Esquilino market. He served what the neighbourhood recognised: egg pasta, slow-cooked meat, loose wine from the Castelli.

Sixty years on the room is the same — bordeaux panelling, sand-coloured linen, low light — but the kitchen has grown precise. We shop every day at the piazza Vittorio market, use only meat from Lazio farms, and keep about twenty dishes on the menu, not one more.

We are not after surprises: we are after the right dish, made properly, served without haste.

Chiara & Marco Sabatini third generation

The way we work

Three things, always the same

The market, every morning

Vegetables and fish come from piazza Vittorio, the meat from the Roman countryside. If a product does not convince us, that dish is not on that day.

Lazio in the glass

Eighty labels chosen among Cesanese, Frascati Superiore and small growers from Cori, with a by-the-glass selection that changes every week.

One room only

Forty-two covers, no forced second sitting. A booked table is yours for the whole evening.

From 6.30 pm

The hour of the aperitivo

Before dinner the bar fills up: people off the train at Termini with a suitcase, people coming from the market, people booked for half past eight who would rather not wait sitting down.

Spritz made properly, Turin vermouth with orange peel, Negroni in a tumbler, and a glass of Cesanese for those who prefer wine. There is always something to go with it: small supplì, coppiette, pecorino, sweet Gaeta olives.

We toast standing up every day except Monday, 6.30 – 8.30 pm
